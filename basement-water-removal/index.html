Walking downstairs into standing water is about as stressful as home ownership gets. Heavy rain that overloads the drainage, a dead sump pump, a failed water heater, a broken pipe and foundation seepage all put water downstairs, where it has to be dealt with by professionals right away before it damages the structure, grows mold and destroys what you store there. For basement emergencies in Akron, Akron Restoration Co is on call 24/7 with strong pumps and modern drying equipment, working to make the space safe and dry as fast as we can.

Drying a basement is its own challenge: the space is below ground level, air barely circulates and dampness lingers. Moisture gets trapped by the surrounding soil, the poured floor and the block walls, so it will not evaporate naturally. To handle that, our crew uses equipment made for the job, including submersible pumps where the water is deep, truck-mounted units when there is a lot of it, and carefully placed dehumidifiers with air movers built for drying below ground.

Getting the water out is step one, and tracking down why the basement flooded is how we help prevent a repeat. Our technicians inspect the sump pump system, foundation drainage, window wells, wall penetrations and grading to identify what contributed. From there you get our recommendations for lasting fixes, which may include waterproofing, interior drainage or installing a backup sump pump to guard the basement against the next intrusion.

Signs You Need Basement Water Removal in Akron

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Basement flooring, carpet or tile that is discolored or breaking down
  • Standing water on your basement floor, whatever the source
  • Foundation walls that feel wet, most often near the bottom
  • Sump pump trouble: it never shuts off, never kicks on or sounds wrong
  • Cracks in the floor, seams between wall and floor or gaps around pipes letting water in
  • Water pooling in window wells or leaking in around basement windows
  • Rust on the base of the furnace, water heater or metal support posts

How to Prevent Basement Water Removal Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Check that the ground next to the house still slopes away, and regrade where it has settled
  • Keep window wells clear of debris and consider adding window well covers
  • Place leak sensors by the water heater and sump pit so you hear about water early
  • Keep gutters flowing freely to stop roof water from spilling next to basement walls
  • Cracks showing in the slab or foundation walls should be patched with hydraulic cement
  • Test your sump pump quarterly by pouring water into the pit and confirming it runs
